**Podium 0.1.5.2** adds Jellyfin music libraries to the Podium wheel.

### What's new
- **Jellyfin Edition:** browse music by folder, album, artist and playlist; sync and cache the library from Settings.
- **Track actions:** long-press the centre button on a track to play it next, enqueue it or add it to a playlist.
- **Português (Brasil):** choose the app language in Settings.
- **Updates from this fork:** check and install compatible signed releases from the Jellyfin Edition repository.

### Fixed
- **Now Playing quality:** keep codec and bitrate together on one line.
- **Mini player X:** stop playback when closing the mini player.
